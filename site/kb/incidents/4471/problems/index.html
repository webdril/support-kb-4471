The problems

parser: the shared parser reads dates month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first. A booking for 4 July goes out as 7 April. It reaches the desk through the app, by email, and through booking-v2’s form, whose hint (DD/MM/YYYY) is right.

hint: booking-v3’s form tells day-first tenants to type MM/DD/YYYY. People do what the hint says, and the form then reads their input the tenant’s way, day first. The report looks the same as a parser report, a day/month swap, but it only comes through the form, only at tenants on booking-v3, and the fix is the hint, not the parser. Fixing the parser alone leaves it in place.

shift: the date moves by one day. Tuesday 12 May shows as Monday 11 May; a client in Lisbon gets Tuesday while a client in Toronto gets Monday; an invite arrives before its booking. No day and month swap here: the time is converted across zones and the day boundary moves. The desk noted these in desk-notes.json and still filed them under parser by tenant. The parser fix won’t touch them.
